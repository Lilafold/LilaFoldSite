LilaFold Labs is an early-stage research company started by three founders. We are building small, specialised AI models, a cloud to serve them, and, later, modular hardware to run them on.

This post is the short version of what that means and the order we are doing it in.

## Three folds of one idea

Our logo is a ribbon folded three times, and so is the plan.

1. **Specialised models.** Compact models trained for one field each. The first is for cybersecurity.
2. **LilaFold Cloud.** Our models behind a simple API, with usage pricing and subscriptions, so you can use them without managing any hardware.
3. **Modular hardware.** Compute modules you can start with one of and add to as your workload grows. This part is in research.

## Why this order

Models come first because everything else depends on them being good. The cloud comes next because it is the fastest way to put them in people's hands and learn from real use. Hardware comes last, once the models and the demand are proven, because building hardware is slow and expensive and we want to get it right.

## Where we are today

We are at the start. The first model is in development, the cloud is not open yet and there is no hardware for sale. We would rather say that plainly than dress it up.
